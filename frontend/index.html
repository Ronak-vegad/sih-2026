Transparent by design

How answers are built

A retrieval-augmented pipeline with a hard guardrail at the end.

01

Your question is classified

The router decides whether you need a deterministic QCO/standard lookup, a procedure explanation, or a general FAQ answer — and refuses anything outside BIS scope.

02

Official sources are retrieved

Passages from BIS FAQs, QCO notifications and certification procedures are pulled using hybrid keyword (BM25) + semantic vector search, fused by reciprocal rank.

03

Answer is grounded & verified

The model writes only from retrieved context. Any IS number missing from the sources is stripped before you see it, and the exact documents used are listed.

When confidence is low, the assistant says so instead of guessing — and points you to bis.gov.in or the BIS CARE helpline 1800-11-4000.

Good to know

Common questions

Where does the information come from?

Public BIS material — FAQ pages, certification and hallmarking procedure documents, and Quality Control Order notifications — collected and indexed offline. Product-to-standard mappings are additionally kept in a structured table so they can be answered deterministically.

Can it invent an IS standard number?

No. After generation, every IS number in the draft answer is checked against the retrieved text and the structured QCO records. Anything that doesn't appear there is replaced with a verification notice rather than shown to you.

Is this an official BIS service?

No. It is a prototype built for the Smart India Hackathon (problem statement SIH26107). It helps you find and understand official information faster, but regulatory decisions must always be confirmed against bis.gov.in or BIS CARE.

What happens if my question is out of scope?

The classifier detects non-BIS topics (tax, legal advice, general trivia) and declines politely with a pointer to the right channel, instead of producing a confident-sounding but unusable answer.
